Why This Exists

The Invocation is a clear, memorable way for people to assert their legal rights in encounters with police. Inspired by case law and the need for concise self-protection, it distills vital constitutional protections into one spoken phrase.

Like "stop, drop, and roll," it's designed to be known by everyone. It's not legal advice—it's legal defense through clarity and culture.
